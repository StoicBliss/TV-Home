# Wallpaper Credits

The launcher dynamically rotates curated high-resolution landscape wallpapers fetched from Unsplash (under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license)) across diverse nature themes:

- **Birds**: Bald eagles in flight and perched, seabirds soaring coastal cliffs, keel-billed toucans, songbirds, flamingos at sunrise, and Atlantic puffins.
- **Wildlife**: Grizzly bears in mountain streams, giant pandas in bamboo forests, red foxes in autumn woods, wolves in wilderness mist, polar bears on Arctic ice, leopards on acacia trees, brown bear cubs, cheetahs on the savannah, and sea turtles in coral reefs.
- **Mountains**: Rocky summits, snowy alpine peaks, starry night peaks, and Norwegian fjord lakes.
- **Forests**: Sunlit woodland canopies, misty pine forests, morning redwood mist, and golden autumn trails.
- **Rivers & Waterfalls**: Autumn river reflections, cascading streams, and alpine rapids.
- **Valleys**: Yosemite Valley iconic river vista, green rolling hills, wildflower valleys at sunset, and coastal sunset vistas.

All wallpapers are strictly wide landscape orientation (aspect ratio $\ge$ 16:9 or 3:2) and dynamically sized to the device's display resolution (including 4K UHD and 1080p Full HD).
