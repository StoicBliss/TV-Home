# Wallpaper Credits

The launcher dynamically rotates curated high-resolution wallpapers fetched from Unsplash (under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license)) across diverse nature themes:

- **Birds**: Bald eagles in flight and perched, colorful songbirds, barn owls, hummingbirds, tropical macaws, seabirds, and toucans.
- **Wildlife**: Lions in savannas and golden hour, tigers in jungle pools, stags in misty woods, giraffes, African elephants, leopards in acacia trees, grizzly bears, and zebras.
- **Mountains**: Rocky peaks, snowy summits, alpine lakes, mountain ridge sunsets, and starry peaks.
- **Forests**: Sunlit woods, misty pine forests, redwood canopies, and deep autumn trails.
- **Rivers & Waterfalls**: Canyon waterfalls, autumn river rapids, cascading woodland falls, and emerald rivers.
- **Valleys**: Yosemite Valley, alpine valley lakes, emerald canyons, and rolling green hills.
